Research

Generative models and computer vision for 3D medical imaging.

I develop and evaluate controllable generative models for 3D medical imaging, combining diffusion models, computer vision and multimodal learning to integrate images, text, anatomy and clinical information.

About

A personal perspective on research and collaboration.

My work sits where computer science, artificial intelligence and medicine meet. I like research that is technically rigorous, but always connected to the question of why a model should matter in practice.

I especially enjoy interdisciplinary collaborations, teaching and MSc supervision — and I value the different way of thinking that comes from having trained in both AI and biomedical engineering.
